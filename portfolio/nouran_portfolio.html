OlistIQ — AI-Powered BI Platform

Product Overview

An AI-powered business intelligence ecosystem designed to transform complex transactional data into intelligent executive insights.

OlistIQ processes 100,000+ records across 8 relational tables through a 5-stage ETL pipeline, delivering 10 analytical modules with automated anomaly detection and Google Gemini-powered conversational analytics — all in one unified platform.

⬡ GitHub Repository ⬢ Live Demo

⚠ The Business Problem

Enterprises drowning in fragmented transactional data — slow reporting cycles, siloed analytics, zero conversational access to insights, and traditional dashboards that show what but never why.

Analysts spending 80% of their time on data preparation instead of generating value. Decision-makers waiting weeks for reports.

✦ The OlistIQ Solution

A modular OOP architecture that automates the entire analytics pipeline — from raw CSV ingestion to AI-generated executive insights. Stakeholders ask questions in plain English; OlistIQ answers with data.

Business intelligence that speaks human. Analytics that think. Dashboards that explain themselves.
